Everything on screen is averaged over this window — the colour, the surface line, the overlay and every instrument. The line sits at the averaged surface level; the depth is the averaged water depth. In an aerated column the two disagree, and the gap printed above is the difference.
The window restarts on R, a drawn edit, a valve, a celerity change and the end of spin-up — and on anything that opens or closes an edge, under Controls → Flow or → Boundaries. Those read as settings, but an edge is a wall of the control volume.
